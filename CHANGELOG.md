# Changelog

## v0.19 — Reliability and polish

- Clearer card inspection with lighter background blur.
- More reliable discard choices and reconnect recovery.
- Safer pack opening and groundwork for server-validated rewards.

## v0.18 — Cards and practice

- Shared card inspection across the game and combat history.
- Independent difficulty and deck choices for practice matches.
- Improved mobile feedback, card presentation and navigation.

## v0.17 — Interface refresh

- Redesigned home screen, deck editor and saved-deck previews.
- More consistent card artwork, proportions and readable text.
- Improved mobile hands, tutorials, targeting and selection controls.

## Base-set reset — March 2026

- Returned to the five base decks: Fish, Reptile, Amphibian, Bird and Mammal.
- Older notes below include retired experimental decks, such as Crustacean and Insect.

## v0.16 — Multiplayer and tutorials

- Host-validated actions, synchronized matches and reconnect recovery.
- Interactive tutorial scenes with animated gameplay examples.
- Separate music, effects and voice controls.

## v0.15 — Deck and keyword polish

- Corrected Crustacean costs, nutrition and Free Spells.
- Refined Evasive, Unstoppable and Insect multi-strike mechanics.
- Expanded metamorphosis effects and status tooltips.

## v0.14 — AI difficulty

- Simplified practice difficulty to Easy and True Sim.
- True Sim uses deeper game-tree search.
- Existing difficulty selections adapt to the new choices.

## v0.13 — Keyword fixes

- Corrected Stalk, Toxic, Neurotoxic and Hidden/Lure interactions.
- Fixed missing feline effects and pride-wide buffs.
- Strengthened Poisonous and Shell/Molt regression tests.

## v0.12 — Experimental Insects

- Introduced the Insect tribe and larva-to-adult metamorphosis.
- Added colony synergies for ants and bees.
- Built card effects around distinctive insect behaviors.

## v0.11 — Code cleanup

- Removed duplicate targeting definitions.
- Fixed browser-environment handling in the deck builder.

## v0.1 — Version tracking

- Added the home-screen version label and changelog.
